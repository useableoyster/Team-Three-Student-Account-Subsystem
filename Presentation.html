Student Account Portal 01
01 / Project Overview

Project Purpose

Our goal was to create a central location where students can easily access and understand their account information.

  • View tuition and fees.
  • Keep track of payments.
  • Check financial aid information.
  • Access account information in one place.
Student Account Portal 02
02 / Website Design

Our Website Design

We designed the website to be simple, organized, and easy to navigate.

Simplicity

A clean layout without unnecessary information.

Organization

Account information separated into clear sections.

Accessibility

Important information is easy to find.

Convenience

Students can access multiple account features in one place.
